# OKRs — case outline (promoted)

**Status:** Gallery card live as **OKRs** (`/?project=okrs`) · badge `Enterprise case · OKRs`  
**Context:** Enterprise PPM at Marketplace scale  
**Evidence bar:** Shipped enterprise product work. +47% needs Impact footnote (team outcome).

## Problem
Leadership teams struggled to connect strategy to execution. OKRs lived in slides or disconnected tools; the module needed a hierarchy leaders would actually open during planning — goals → key results → work — without drowning in Jira noise.

## Role
Lead Product Designer through Head of Design and Senior UX Manager eras. Partnered with PM and engineering on IA, dense hierarchy UX, and adoption rituals.

## Decision
Bet on a **strategy-to-task hierarchy** leaders can scan in weekly rituals — clear ownership, progress, and drill-down — instead of a decorative OKR board that mirrors PowerPoint.

## Outcome
- Reported: **+47% OKR adoption among leadership teams** (team outcome — see Impact)
- Cover + hierarchy / health / empty screens exported from Stitch
