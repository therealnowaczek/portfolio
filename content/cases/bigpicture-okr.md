## Problem
Leadership teams struggled to connect strategy to execution inside BigPicture. OKRs lived in slides or disconnected tools; the module needed a hierarchy leaders would actually open during planning — goals → key results → work — without drowning in Jira noise.

## Role
Principal / lead craft through Head of Design and Senior UX Manager eras (confirm exact ownership window in write-up). Partnered with PM and engineering on IA, dense hierarchy UX, and adoption rituals.

## Decision
Bet on a **strategy-to-task hierarchy** leaders can scan in weekly rituals — clear ownership, progress, and drill-down — instead of a decorative OKR board that mirrors PowerPoint.

## Outcome (needs sourcing before bare claim)
- Reported: **+47% OKR adoption among leadership teams**
- Required in final case: baseline adoption %, measurement window, cohort definition, and what was personal vs team vs product marketing.
